Besseresser – your personal AI nutrition companion for Android.

No calorie counting. No endless food databases. Just snap a photo of your plate and get an honest traffic-light rating (Green / Yellow / Red) for each of your personal nutrition goals within seconds.

<b>How it works</b>
* Define what matters to you once, e.g. "Lower blood pressure", "Less sugar", "Lose weight" or "More protein".
* Take a photo of your meal (or describe it in text if you prefer).
* Besseresser sends the image to your own AI provider (e.g. OpenRouter / DeepSeek) using <i>your</i> API key and rates the meal against every one of your goals.
* Each goal gets a traffic-light rating plus a short, honest explanation.

<b>Meal history &amp; daily summaries</b>
* Every rated meal is stored in a visual history – including the recognized food, your notes and the photo thumbnail.
* Once a day (after 20:00) or on demand, the AI sums up your whole day as a "Tagesfazit" (day summary). Weekly summaries ("Wochenfazit") with the calendar week are generated on demand.
* Meals can be duplicated to another date, deleted with an undo window, and reordered within a day by long-pressing.

<b>Favorites &amp; notes</b>
* Notes you write on meals are remembered, can be starred and are suggested again in similar situations.

<b>Privacy &amp; data</b>
* All data stays on your device. Besseresser contains no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
* Photos and prompts are sent only to the AI provider you configure, with your own API key. Your goals are never uploaded beyond what a rating requires.

<b>Available in English and German</b> – the interface and the AI evaluations automatically adapt to your language.
